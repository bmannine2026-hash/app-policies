Privacy Policy - Facts Channel Poster

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Facts Channel Poster is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single individual for the sole purpose of posting pre-produced short-form video content to that same individual's own TikTok account. It is not a public service, and no other TikTok users or accounts interact with it.

What data this app accesses

This app uses TikTok's Content Posting API, authorized via TikTok's own OAuth flow, to upload and publish video files to the developer's own TikTok account. It does not access, read, or store data belonging to any other TikTok user, and it does not collect analytics, personal information, or content from TikTok's platform beyond what is required to publish the developer's own videos.

What data is stored

An OAuth access token issued by TikTok is stored locally on the developer's own machine to authorize video uploads. This token is never shared with, transmitted to, or accessible by any third party. Video files uploaded through the app are produced entirely by the developer and contain no third-party personal data.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. This app does not use tracking, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics services.
