Facts Channel Poster

Facts Channel Poster is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single developer to publish their own pre-produced short-form educational video content to their own TikTok account, using TikTok's Content Posting API.

What it does

The tool takes finished, pre-produced vertical videos — short factual and educational clips, fully scripted, narrated, and captioned ahead of time — and publishes them directly to the developer's own TikTok account. There is no manual upload step: the app authenticates once via TikTok's OAuth flow, then calls the Content Posting API to publish.

Who uses it

This is a single-user tool. It is used exclusively by its developer, on their own TikTok account, under their own TikTok developer credentials. It is not distributed, resold, or made available to any other individual or organization, and no other TikTok account or user data is ever accessed by the app.

Content

All published content is original or produced from properly licensed or public-domain source material by the developer, and complies with TikTok's Community Guidelines, including disclosure of AI-generated content where applicable.
